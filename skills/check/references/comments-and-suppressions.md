# Review comments and suppressions

Compare their claims with the implementation. Preserve useful intent, compatibility constraints, external limitations, and required license or tooling directives. Apply the [comment policy](../../pancake/references/comment-cleanup.md) to changed code comments. Report redundant narration and other policy violations separately from correctness bugs. A read-only review does not authorize removal.

When a comment claims a requirement, such as ordering, exclusive ownership, or exact wording, trace why it matters and whether code, types, tests, or tooling enforce it. A comment can describe a real constraint even when enforcement is absent. Report a missing safeguard only with a concrete triggering scenario and consequence, separating confirmed breakage from a proposed improvement. Recommend the smallest appropriate enforcement within scope; a review does not authorize implementing it or deleting the constraint's record.

For lint or type-check suppressions, inspect the diagnostic being suppressed, the reason, and the guarded behavior. Distinguish a justified external limitation from a hidden defect or stale workaround. Do not assume that a suppression is wrong or that removing it preserves behavior. When recurring workarounds suggest a faulty design, describe the shared premise and affected callers rather than proposing another exception. Leave optional cleanup separate from correctness findings and keep it scoped to the changed code or explicitly requested audit.
